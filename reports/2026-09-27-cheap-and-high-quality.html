Method notes

  • Quality: six-metric 0–100 score (ROIC 25%, FCF margin 20%, cash quality 15%, leverage 15%, EBITDA growth 15%, revenue growth 10%), percentile-ranked across the whole pool. Source: quality pipeline, annual statements.
  • EV/EBITDA (norm.): computed in this report from the valuation pipeline's quarterly files; reported is the pipeline's own figure. P/E is TTM on reported net income, so it carries the same gains distortion (GOOG and CRM P/E look cheaper than their operations).
  • Forward P/E is Yahoo's analyst consensus (next twelve months): it moves with revisions and is not audited.
  • Technical: TradingView-style vote of 10 moving averages + 6 oscillators on daily closes. Momentum only — not a view on the business.
  • Peers are limited to companies in the screened universe (25 largest per sector, USD reporters). Several natural competitors — Paychex, Workday, Canva, Figma, H&R Block, Amazon — are not in it, so peer comparisons are partial.
  • Quarterly charts show at most five quarters (what Yahoo returns), so year-over-year growth is available only for the latest quarter.
  • Palette: the three documented-validated categorical slots (blue, orange, aqua); the palette validator could not be run locally (no Node in the container).
