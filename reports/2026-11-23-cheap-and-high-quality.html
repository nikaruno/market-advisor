Method notes

  • Quality: six-metric 0–100 score (ROIC 25%, FCF margin 20%, cash quality 15%, leverage 15%, EBITDA growth 15%, revenue growth 10%), each metric percentile-ranked across the whole pool, from the annual-statement pipeline. Growth is ranked on amplitude, not durability — a company at a cyclical peak scores highly for the same reason it is about to disappoint. MU below is the clearest live example.
  • EV/EBITDA (norm.): computed in this report from the quarterly statements, on Normalized TTM EBITDA. The pipeline's own ranking uses reported EBITDA and is shown alongside.
  • Technical: a vote of 10 moving averages and 6 oscillators — momentum only, never a quality judgement. Earlier runs in this project showed these labels flipping between Strong Buy and Strong Sell on sub-1% price moves, so treat them as context, not signal.
  • Blank ratios stay blank. A loss-maker gets no P/E and no EV/EBITDA rather than an estimate.
  • Forward P/E is an analyst consensus, moves, and is not TTM.
